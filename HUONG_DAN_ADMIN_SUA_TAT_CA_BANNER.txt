HUONG DAN ADMIN SUA TAT CA BANNER

1. Chep thu muc public vao thu muc du an.
2. Publish lai firestore.rules va storage.rules.
3. Chay chay-thu-web.bat, dang nhap tai khoan role=admin.
4. Vao Quan tri he thong > Giao dien.
5. O Trinh quan ly toan bo banner, chon banner trong danh sach.
6. Sua nhan, tieu de, mo ta, bieu tuong, anh nen, vi tri va do toi.
7. Bam Luu noi dung hoac Tai anh va ap dung.

Banner ho tro:
- Dang nhap va dang ky
- Trung tam quan tri
- Toan bo banner dau trang cua hoc phan, de thi, bai nop, diem, trac nghiem, lop truc tuyen va tin nhan
- Banner chao Dashboard van co trinh sua chuyen biet ngay ben duoi

Luu y: banner canh bao dong (qua han, loi mang, trang thai nop bai) khong cho sua noi dung vi day la thong tin nghiep vu tu dong.
