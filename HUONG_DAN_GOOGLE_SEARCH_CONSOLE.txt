CAC BUOC BAN PHAI TU LAM SAU KHI DEPLOY

1. Deploy lai thu muc source nay len Vercel.
2. Kiem tra 3 dia chi sau mo duoc:
   https://lmskhoadientu.vercel.app/
   https://lmskhoadientu.vercel.app/robots.txt
   https://lmskhoadientu.vercel.app/sitemap.xml
3. Mo Google Search Console va them property dang URL prefix:
   https://lmskhoadientu.vercel.app/
4. Chon xac minh bang HTML tag. Google se cap mot the dang:
   <meta name="google-site-verification" content="MA_XAC_MINH" />
5. Dan the do vao trong <head> cua public/index.html, deploy lai, sau do bam Verify.
6. Trong Search Console, mo URL Inspection, nhap URL trang chu va bam Request Indexing.
7. Vao muc Sitemaps, nhap sitemap.xml va bam Submit.
8. Sau mot thoi gian, kiem tra tren Google bang:
   site:lmskhoadientu.vercel.app

Luu y: Khong the dien san ma google-site-verification vi ma nay chi duoc Google cap cho tai khoan cua ban.
